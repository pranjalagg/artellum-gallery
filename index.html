Hello, I’m the hands behind Artellum.

I paint, crochet and make small things for walls from a corner of my home that is slowly turning into a studio. Everything here is made by me, by hand.

Paintings and most wall pieces are one of a kind. The small things are made to order, so they can be made and customized just for you.
